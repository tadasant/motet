brand idea

Motet turns content you trust into an interactive podcast you can listen to on the go.

The content you trust — the newsletters, bookmarks and threads you personally chose to follow — becomes one interactive podcast, listened to on the move. So the brand makes depth visible, and then makes it beautiful. Several trusted streams resolve into one clean signal that runs at the surface; when something catches you, you ask, and the signal steps down a level, then another, then returns to the episode. The aesthetic is a well-made instrument: dark, precise, unhurried, with a single electric line that marks where you went deeper.

Lime is spent on exactly three things — your voice and the dive it opens, the live indicator, and the one action we want you to take. Everything else is grey, so that the accent means something when it appears.
