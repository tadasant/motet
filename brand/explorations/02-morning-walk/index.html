Direction 02 · Morning Walk
Brand idea

Motet turns content you trust into an interactive podcast you can listen to on the go.

Motet lives outdoors, at the hour you are not at a screen. It is a ritual brand for learning on the go, not a piece of software: natural light, breathing room, one unhurried voice turning the newsletters, bookmarks and threads you chose to follow into a podcast that walks with you. Those sources are still there — they sit quietly in the layers of the landscape, hill behind hill — and the walk itself is the episode.

The opposite of a dashboard. Nothing blinks, nothing counts up, nothing asks you to come back. But the podcast is interactive, and the route is yours to wander: say what caught your ear and it steps off the path, goes deeper, then rejoins where it left off. The product is finished when the walk is.

Sand is the ground, moss carries every action, sky is light rather than surface. Terracotta is the sun: used once or twice a page, never as a second brand colour. The hill tints between moss and sky are derived, not named.

Plus Jakarta Sans 800 · letter-spacing −0.04em · line-height 0.98

Source Sans 3 carries the body. It is warm, open and unfussy at reading sizes, which is what a brand about listening rather than looking needs from its running text. Sentences are allowed to be long, because they will be spoken, not skimmed: this is copy for an episode, not a screen. Numbers sit in tabular figures so a route time reads the same at every waypoint.
