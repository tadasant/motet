How it works

Three movements, one walk.

No new app to read, no inbox to clear. The sources you already chose become something you can take out the door — and talk to.

  1. i

    Bring the content you trust.

    Start with the newsletters you already read. Bookmarks and threads join them next. Trust comes from you having picked them.

  2. ii

    Listen on the go.

    Motet sings them as one episode for a walk, a commute or the dishes. When three sources cover the same story, you hear it once.

  3. iii

    Just ask.

    When a story catches you, say so out loud. Motet goes deeper on it, then picks the episode back up where it left off.

The idea

Why motet.

A motet is a choral form in which several texts are sung at once and resolve into one coherent piece.

Motet does the same with the newsletters, bookmarks and threads you chose to follow: many voices you already trust, sung together into one podcast you take with you. Short, declarative, and yours to interrupt.
