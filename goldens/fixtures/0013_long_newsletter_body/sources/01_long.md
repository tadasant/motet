Thorn releases its annual transparency report. The company published the
report on Tuesday morning alongside a blog post from its general counsel.

The report covers requests received between January and December. Government
requests rose by nineteen per cent year over year, with the largest share
coming from three jurisdictions. Thorn said it rejected or narrowed just over
a third of the requests it received.

Civil society groups gave the report a mixed reception. One coalition welcomed
the added detail on account preservation, while another said the aggregate
figures still make it impossible to tell how many individual users were
affected.

Thorn says the next edition will break the numbers down by request type.
